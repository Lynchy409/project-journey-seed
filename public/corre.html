¡Corre!

A Spanish deathrun. The words decide where you run: pick the right arch, obey the shouted command, or die and start again. Words swap doors on every attempt, so you can't memorise the route, only the language.

← → or A DChange lane (swipe sideways) ↑ W or SpaceJump (swipe up or tap) ↓ or SDuck (swipe down) EHear the audio again (tap the top of the screen)

Mis palabras

Add your own words, one per line: the Spanish, then =, then an emoji or the English meaning. An emoji is better, because a picture links to meaning without going through English.

Voz

The game uses the Spanish voices built into your device, and quality varies a lot. Try each one and keep the most natural.

Zona tranquila

New words. Look, listen, take your time. Tap any card to hear it again. Next, they decide which arch you run through.

¡Muerto!
